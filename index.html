Mochi Cat

The cat who reads with you.

A tiny pixel-art cat that walks on the actual lines of text on the page you're reading — no accounts, no ads, no network requests, ever.

🔒 Zero network requests 🧘 Never interrupts your reading 🌐 English & Türkçe 🆓 Free, no accounts

How it works

Unlike a screen pet that floats aimlessly on top of the page, Mochi understands the structure of what you're reading. It walks along sentences, perches on headings, hops between paragraphs, and curls up on images. Right-click to send it away for 10 minutes, 30, an hour, or the rest of the day — or press Alt+Shift+C (⌥⇧C on Mac) to hide it instantly, anywhere.

Privacy, honestly

Mochi makes zero network requests. It has no analytics, no accounts, and no remote code. Everything it remembers — your cat's name, its coat, your site preferences — stays in chrome.storage.local, on your machine, never synced or sent anywhere. Read the full privacy policy.
